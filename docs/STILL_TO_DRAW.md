## How to bring a hidden reward back

1. Drop the picture in its folder.
2. Ask for it to be wired. The reward goes into `data/art_map.json` → `rewards`, and `"hidden": true` comes off in `data/rewards.json`.

Children who already own a hidden reward keep it in their save. It shows again once its picture is wired.
