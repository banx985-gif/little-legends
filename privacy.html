Little Legends: Magic World

Privacy policy, version 1.1 (October 2026)

Everything stays on your tablet. Little Legends never sends anything over the internet.

Little Legends is a learning game for children aged 2–5. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no ads and no trackers. The Android app has the whole game inside it and asks for no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access.

What the game keeps on the tablet

  • Child profiles: a first name or nickname, an age (2–5), a language and a favourite colour.
  • Play progress: which missions are finished and where a mission was left, Discovery Stars and eggs.
  • Rewards and island: friends, Pip’s looks, decorations and rides, and where things stand on Wonder Island.
  • Learning record: which skills were practised, how many tries were right or needed another go, and hints. It is used only to choose the next activity and to show the grown-up’s learning page.
  • Family settings: volumes, quiet mode, motion, text size, colour symbols, break reminder and picture quality.
  • Grown-up tools (only if used): test-session notes and the release checklist in the Parent Area.
  • Game files: the game’s own pictures and code, kept so it works offline. These contain no personal data.

This is saved in the app’s (or browser’s) own storage on the tablet. It is not backed up to the cloud by the app.

What the game never collects

Email addresses, photos, voice recordings, location, contacts, device IDs, advertising IDs or usage analytics. The game never asks for the camera, microphone or location. Spoken instructions use the tablet’s own speech voice.

Children’s safety

  • No ads and no purchases. Discovery Stars are earned by playing and cannot be bought.
  • No chat, sharing or public profiles.
  • No links out of the game. The Parent Area is behind a hold-to-open Parent Gate.

Your control

In the Parent Area (hold the gate), the DATA tab can save a copy of everything as a file, or delete everything from the tablet. Uninstalling the app also deletes all of it.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page and in the game’s Parent Area → PRIVACY. For questions, use the developer contact details on the game’s Google Play page.
